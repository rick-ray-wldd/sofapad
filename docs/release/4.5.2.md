# SofaPad 4.5.2

Use your iPhone as the Mac's keyboard and trackpad — from the sofa, or with Vision Pro and Mac Virtual Display. The
phone opens a page served by a small menu-bar app on the Mac; nothing goes through a cloud.

## What's new

- **A compatibility fix for the squeezed page when typing on an upright iPhone.** On an iPhone with an iOS 27 beta,
  tapping the text box squeezed the page into the top of the screen: the landscape layout with a narrow trackpad,
  "Tap here to type" cut off on a second line, the last key row cut in half, and an empty band above the keyboard.
  The phone page asked the browser to resize the whole page for the keyboard, a request iOS used to ignore. If a
  browser starts honoring it, the page can become wider than tall and get the landscape layout; that matches the
  screenshot, but it was not confirmed on the reported iPhone. The page now asks only for the visible part to
  shrink, as it was designed. In the checks below, the controls sit right above the keyboard with the trackpad
  above them. A check on the affected beta iPhone is still to come.
- The page also notices the keyboard again on iOS 27, so no empty strip is kept for the home indicator while you
  type.

Nothing else changed: the Mac app and the protocol are as in 4.5.1. After the update, the phone page reloads itself
to 4.5.2.

## Download and install

`SofaPad-4.5.2-macOS.zip` — macOS 13 or later, **Apple silicon (arm64) only**. On an Intel Mac, build it yourself.
Check it with `shasum -a 256 -c SofaPad-4.5.2-macOS.zip.sha256`.

**New install**: unzip it, drag `SofaPad.app` into Applications and double-click it. macOS asks whether you want to
open an app downloaded from the internet: click **Open**. Then turn on the Accessibility permission. Full steps, in
English and Chinese: [`docs/INSTALL.md`](../INSTALL.md).

**From 4.5.1**: SofaPad offers 4.5.2 by itself (or ⌨︎🖱 → 檢查更新…, Check for Updates): **Install Update**, then
**Install and Relaunch**. The Accessibility permission stays. The phone page reloads itself.

**From 4.5.0**: the same, then give the Accessibility permission one last time: System Settings > Privacy & Security >
Accessibility, select SofaPad, click **−**, then click **+** and add `/Applications/SofaPad.app`.

**From 4.4 or earlier**: quit SofaPad, put this one in its place in Applications, open it, and give it the
Accessibility permission again the same way.

## Known limits

- The fix was checked in a simulated phone page (headless Chrome, iPhone sizes, the keyboard replayed the way the
  browser handles the page's viewport setting) and in the iOS 27.0 simulator, which shows 4.5.1 correctly too. It has
  not been checked on the affected iOS 27 beta iPhone yet. The empty band above the keyboard in the report is not
  explained and not known to be gone.
- Plain http on the local network, no encryption: use it at home or on a network you trust.

# SofaPad 4.5.2（繁體中文）

把 iPhone 當成 Mac 的鍵盤和觸控板：在沙發上用，或戴 Vision Pro 用 Mac 虛擬顯示器時用。手機打開 Mac 選單列小程式提供的網頁，不經過任何雲端。

## 這一版

- **針對直拿打字時畫面被擠壓的相容性修正。** 在裝 iOS 27 beta 的 iPhone 上，點輸入框後畫面會被擠到上方：變成橫拿的版面，觸控板只剩窄窄一條，「Tap here to type」換成兩行而且被切掉，最下排按鍵被切一半，鍵盤上面空出一大塊。手機網頁一直要求瀏覽器「鍵盤出現時把整個頁面縮小」，以前 iOS 不理這個設定；如果瀏覽器開始照做，頁面可能變得比寬還矮、改用橫拿版面。這和截圖相符，但還沒在回報的那支 iPhone 上證實。現在改成只讓看得到的範圍縮小，回到原本的設計；在下面的檢查裡，直拿打字時控制區貼在鍵盤正上方，觸控板在控制區上面。受影響的 beta 真機還要確認。
- 在 iOS 27 上，網頁又能認出鍵盤開著了，打字時不會在下方留一條空白。

其他都沒有變：Mac App 和協定都和 4.5.1 一樣。更新後，手機網頁會自己重新載入成 4.5.2。

## 下載和安裝

`SofaPad-4.5.2-macOS.zip`：macOS 13 以上，**只支援 Apple 晶片（arm64）**；Intel Mac 請自己 build。可以用 `shasum -a 256 -c SofaPad-4.5.2-macOS.zip.sha256` 核對。

**第一次安裝**：解壓縮，把 `SofaPad.app` 拖到「應用程式」，再雙擊打開。macOS 問要不要打開從網路下載的 App 時，按「**打開**」。接著打開「輔助使用」權限。完整步驟（中英）：[`docs/INSTALL.md`](../INSTALL.md)。

**從 4.5.1 更新**：SofaPad 會自己提示 4.5.2（或點 ⌨︎🖱 →「檢查更新…」），按「**安裝更新項目**」，再按「**安裝並重新啟動**」。「輔助使用」權限會保留，手機網頁會自己重新載入。

**從 4.5.0 更新**：步驟一樣，裝好後最後一次重新開「輔助使用」權限：系統設定 → 隱私權與安全性 → 輔助使用，選 SofaPad 按「**－**」移除，再按「**＋**」加入 `/應用程式/SofaPad.app`。

**從 4.4 或更早的版本**：結束 SofaPad，把這一版放到「應用程式」取代舊的，打開它，再用同樣的方法開「輔助使用」權限。

## 已知限制

- 這次的修正是在模擬的手機頁面（headless Chrome，iPhone 尺寸，照瀏覽器處理網頁設定的方式模擬鍵盤）和 iOS 27.0 模擬器上檢查的；4.5.1 在這個模擬器上本來就正常。還沒在受影響的 iOS 27 beta iPhone 上檢查。回報裡鍵盤上方那塊空白的原因還不清楚，也不能確定已經消失。
- 區網內是一般的 http，沒有加密：請在家裡或信任的網路使用。
